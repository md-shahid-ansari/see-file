# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to making participation in the SeeFile community a welcoming, respectful, and harassment-free experience for everyone.

We welcome contributions from people of all backgrounds, experience levels, and identities. Everyone participating in this project is expected to act professionally and respectfully.

## Expected Behaviour

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive community include:

- Being respectful, considerate, and constructive.
- Welcoming newcomers and helping them understand the project.
- Giving and accepting feedback professionally.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.
- Respecting different opinions, experiences, and approaches.
- Keeping discussions relevant and solution-oriented.

## Unacceptable Behaviour

The following behaviours are not acceptable:

- Harassment, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Insulting, threatening, or humiliating language.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or abusive comments.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Unwanted sexual attention or inappropriate conduct.
- Any other behaviour that would reasonably make someone feel unsafe or unwelcome.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all SeeFile community spaces, including:

- GitHub issues and discussions.
- Pull requests and code reviews.
- Project-related communication channels.
- Other spaces where someone is representing SeeFile or its community.

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it to the project maintainers through the repository's GitHub contact options.

Please include relevant details so the maintainers can understand and address the situation.

Reports will be handled respectfully and, where possible, confidentially.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this Code of Conduct.

Depending on the severity and circumstances, maintainers may:

1. Issue a warning.
2. Remove inappropriate comments or contributions.
3. Temporarily restrict participation.
4. Permanently exclude someone from the project community.

Maintainers may take appropriate action when necessary to protect the community.

## Our Responsibility

We aim to maintain a community where people can collaborate, learn, contribute, and build useful software together.

Thank you for helping make SeeFile a respectful and welcoming open-source project.
